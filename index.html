A support ticket, from first message to closed

A “where is my order?” email handled end to end.

Ticket
DEMO-1001
Channel
Email
Topic
Delivery delay
Status

  1. Customer message. “I ordered a backpack 12 days ago and the tracking hasn't updated in 8 days. Can you tell me where it is?”
  2. Investigation. I look up the order in Shopify: paid and fulfilled, with a tracking number. Tracking shows no scan for 8 days. The store's knowledge base (a demo policy) treats 7 days without a scan as lost in transit, so the order qualifies for a reship or a refund.
  3. Reply. I apologize for the wait, explain what the tracking shows, and offer a free reship or a full refund, with a clear date to choose by.
  4. Resolution. The customer picks the reship. I create the replacement order, send the new tracking number, and set the ticket to Pending.
  5. Follow-up. Three days later I check tracking, confirm delivery, close the ticket, and note the carrier delay for the team.

Demonstration sample

Handling an order inquiry: a return request

A customer asks how to return an item that doesn't fit.

Customer

“How do I return the jacket I ordered? It doesn't fit.”

How I handle it

  1. Verify. Find the order in Shopify and confirm the delivery date is inside the return window (a 30-day demo policy).
  2. Check eligibility. Confirm the item isn't marked final sale in the knowledge base.
  3. Reply. Send the return steps and label, and say when the refund will be issued.
  4. Log and follow up. Record the ticket, set a reminder for when the item should arrive, and confirm the refund once it does.

My reply

Hi Sam,

Thanks for letting us know. Your order (DEMO-4410) was delivered 9 days ago, so it's well within our 30-day return window. I've attached a prepaid return label and the packing steps. Once the jacket arrives back with us, your refund goes to your original payment method.

If you'd prefer a different size instead, tell me which one and I'll check that it's in stock.

John

My support workflow

How I move a ticket from first message to closed.

  1. InquiryRead the message, identify the issue and how urgent it is, check for earlier tickets from the same customer, and tag the ticket.
  2. InvestigationLook up the order in Shopify, check tracking and order notes, and review the SOP, FAQ, or knowledge base for the right answer.
  3. ResolutionReply clearly with the answer or the fix. If it needs someone else, escalate with a short summary so the customer isn't asked twice.
  4. Follow-upLog the outcome, set a reminder if anything is pending, and check back so the customer isn't left waiting.
